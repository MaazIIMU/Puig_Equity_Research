# Puig Brands SA – Equity Valuation & Investment Analysis

## Overview
This project presents a comprehensive Equity Research and Corporate Valuation analysis of **Puig Brands SA**, a global luxury beauty and fragrance company. The objective of the analysis was to evaluate the intrinsic value of the company using advanced Financial Modeling and Valuation techniques and assess its investment attractiveness.

The project was conducted as part of an MBA coursework assignment in Corporate Finance and Equity Valuation.

---

## Objective
- Analyze Puig Brands SA’s financial and strategic position
- Forecast long-term financial performance
- Estimate intrinsic equity value using DCF Valuation
- Assess investment attractiveness and downside risk
- Develop an institutional-style investment thesis

---

## Methodologies Used

### Financial Analysis
- Revenue Growth Analysis
- Profitability Analysis
- Ratio Analysis
- Capital Structure Analysis
- Geographic Revenue Analysis

### Valuation Techniques
- Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Valuation
- Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF) Modeling
- Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)
- Modified CAPM for Cost of Equity
- Terminal Value Estimation

### Risk & Scenario Analysis
- Monte Carlo Simulation (1,000 iterations)
- Sensitivity Analysis
- Scenario-Based Valuation Analysis

---

## Key Findings

- Estimated Intrinsic Share Price: **EUR 32.33**
- Market Price (20 Aug 2025): **EUR 16.12**
- Estimated Upside Potential: **100.54%**
- Monte Carlo Simulation indicated a high probability of upside potential across modeled scenarios

The analysis suggested that the market was undervaluing Puig Brands SA despite strong fundamentals, premium brand positioning, and long-term growth opportunities in the global beauty and fragrance industry.

---

## Strategic Insights
The investment thesis was supported by:
- Strong global luxury brand portfolio
- Geographic diversification across EMEA, Americas, and APAC
- High-margin premium beauty segments
- Long-term structural growth in fragrance and skincare markets
- Strong profitability and return metrics
